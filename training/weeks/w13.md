# Block week 13 — 2026-08-31 to 2026-09-06

**Champion week 10 — BIG DAY.** The single most important training week of the block. Saturday is
a **true 50k — three out-and-backs on the accessible northern 6.5mi of the course, starting around
18:00 and running much of it in the dark.**

## Why this week looks like this

Champion week 10 restructures the whole week around the Saturday effort: workout moved to Tuesday
as a light tune-up, easy midweek, and NO STRENGTH after Tuesday. That's followed here exactly. The
goal is genuine soreness — this is the dress rehearsal that tells you what breaks before it matters.

Because the athlete has training access to the real course (`athlete/profile.md`), this one session
now does the work of four at once:

1. **The Big Day** — a true 50k at moderately hard effort, which is what the Champion Plan actually
   prescribes rather than the 4h approximation the default budget cap would have forced.
2. **Darkness on tired legs** — an 18:00 start means roughly an hour of fading daylight and then
   the rest in the dark. Running at night isn't itself a stressor for this athlete, but doing it
   at hour 4 of a 50k is a reasonable stand-in for race-day laps 4–5.
3. **Course recon** — the real surface and the real 7.5mi turnaround, hit twice.
4. **Three crew stops** — each out-and-back returns to the staging point, so the crew routine
   (refuel, foot check, sock change, the headlamp-on transition) gets rehearsed three times rather
   than once.

Structured as a **negative split** (6:25 → 6:15 → 6:10/km), which is the pacing discipline the
actual race demands and the single most common thing ultrarunners get wrong.

**Budget exception.** At 5h27 this exceeds `long_run_max_min` (240min) and carries an explicit
`budget_exception:` in its frontmatter — 1 of at most 3 allowed per block. Going long stays
deliberate and rare rather than becoming the default; `scripts/verify_plan.py` enforces the count.
