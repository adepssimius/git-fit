# Block week 13 — 2026-08-31 to 2026-09-06

**Champion week 10 — BIG DAY.** The single most important training week of the block. Saturday is
a **true 50k — three out-and-backs on the accessible northern 6.5mi of the course, starting around
06:00 and finishing near noon**, followed by a 03:00 pre-dawn run on Sunday. The weekend is the
session, not the Saturday alone.

> 🥾 **Vacation week — mountain hiking with the kids.** Several hours on feet on roughly
> half the days, with real vert. That is not a training gap, it's a large time-on-feet and
> eccentric-loading block that the flat course can't otherwise provide — so **planned running
> is cut, not preserved.** Do not run the pre-travel schedule on top of the hiking.
> No course, no trainer, gym uncertain. See `training/block.md` § "Travel weeks".

## Why this week looks like this

Champion week 10 restructures the whole week around the Saturday effort: workout moved to Tuesday
as a light tune-up, easy midweek, and NO STRENGTH after Tuesday. That's followed here exactly. The
goal is genuine soreness — this is the dress rehearsal that tells you what breaks before it matters.

Because the athlete has training access to the real course (`athlete/profile.md`), this one session
now does the work of four at once:

1. **The Big Day** — a true 50k at moderately hard effort, which is what the Champion Plan actually
   prescribes rather than the 4h approximation the default budget cap would have forced.
2. **The setup for Sunday** — finishing around noon is what makes the next morning's 03:00 session
   land on ~5 hours of sleep and 15-hour-old 50k legs. That pairing — fatigue plus sleep pressure
   plus darkness — is the race-lap-5-6 state, and it's the Sunday run that carries the darkness
   rehearsal, not this one. Starting late breaks the weekend's whole design.
3. **Course recon** — the real surface and the real 7.5mi turnaround, hit twice.
4. **Three crew stops** — each out-and-back returns to the staging point, so the crew routine
   (refuel, foot check, sock change, standing rather than sitting) gets rehearsed three times
   rather than once.

Structured as a **negative split** (7:15 → 7:00 → 6:45/km as guides), which is the pacing
discipline the actual race demands and the single most common thing ultrarunners get wrong. This
is the block's **one pace-governed long session** — every other long run follows ZoneSense Zone 1,
but Zone 1 would cap this at an easy aerobic day and the closing 6:45/km is deliberately faster
than `easy_ceiling`. See the exception note in `rules/endurance-authoring.md`.

**Budget exception.** At 6h03 this exceeds `long_run_max_min` (240min) and carries an explicit
`budget_exception:` in its frontmatter — 1 of at most 3 allowed per block. Going long stays
deliberate and rare rather than becoming the default; `scripts/verify_plan.py` enforces the count.

## Verification

- **Running time budget:** 579 min of 600 — OK
  (meeting-time walks and rides excluded, per `rules/progression.md`).
- **Walking ramp:** 220 min meeting-walking (trip ends Tue 09-01, so most of the week is home)
  + 180 min hiking = 400 min combined, down from week 12's ~600 min combined. No ramp concern.
- **Lifting placement:** Lower A (Mon) and Upper A (Tue) only; nothing Wed-Sun, so the Big Day
  is preceded by three consecutive lifting-free days. **Fixed:** Lower A sat the day before
  Tuesday's tune-up (a quality session) with no downgrade — `rules/strength-authoring.md` says
  never heavy lower the day before a quality session, whenever avoidable. Since Champion week 10
  moves the workout to Tuesday specifically, this conflict is structural to the week rather than
  a readiness-ladder call, so Liftoscript Week 5's Lower A is now locked to tier-3 volume
  (accessories cut, mains at a single set) rather than defaulting to tier 1.
- **Budget exception:** 1 of 3 allowed per block, flagged in the session frontmatter.
- **Logistics:** this is an on-course session — add travel time each way on top of the 6h03, and
  stage the three crew stops (refuel, foot check, spare socks) at the car in advance. Sunday's
  03:00 run is the one needing night kit staged, not this.
- **Liftoscript:** week 5 (`liftoscript_week = block_week - 8`).
