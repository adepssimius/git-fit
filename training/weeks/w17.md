Here it becomes lap sim 2: same structure as lap sim 1, but now running the shoes, socks, fuel, crew choreography and night kit **decided last week** (block week 16, using lap sim 1's data) — this is the rehearsal of that kit, not the decision. Anything that goes wrong here still has two weeks to fix.

Saturday's lap simulation is run **on the actual course** — the last of three on-course sessions, and the final chance to fix anything before race day. Budget travel time each way on top of the 2h57.
