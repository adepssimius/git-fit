## Why this week looks like this

Champion week 14 is the last real training week before the taper, and its Saturday is a 'nice effort to open up the nervous system'. Here it becomes lap sim 2: same structure as lap sim 1, but now everything is final — the shoes, socks, fuel, crew choreography and night kit you'll actually use. Anything that goes wrong here still has two weeks to fix.

Saturday's lap simulation is run **on the actual course** — the last of three on-course sessions, and the final chance to fix anything before race day. Budget travel time each way on top of the 2h50.
