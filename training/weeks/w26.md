## The time trial

**Three jobs, and the first one gates four weeks of training:**

1. **Set the real interval paces.** `athlete/zones.yml` has **`vo2: 5:09`, which is 5k race pace**
   from the August PR. His 5k takes ~26 minutes — far longer than the ~6 minutes that defines true
   VO2max pace — **so his actual interval pace is faster than 5:09 by an unknown margin.** The
   entry was adequate only while VO2 work was a garnish. **Weeks 3-6 of this cycle are not
   authored, and cannot be, until this number exists.**
2. **The first high-quality reading of the goal metric.** The watch's `MAXVO2` estimate wants
   sustained hard running with good HR/pace coupling, and a 5k TT is the best input available. The
   standing 48.6 is from August; the 11-15 easy run is a floor.
3. **~25 minutes of hard running in one piece**, as the ramp-in's graduation — roughly one interval
   session's worth, taken as a test with nothing stacked on top of it.

**It is not a PB attempt, and the file says so.** PB is 25:44. Three weeks back from 30 days off,
**a slower time is the expected and useful answer** — the number's job is to be correct, not
flattering.

**The km 1 band (5:35-6:05) is a leash and nothing else.** Go out at it even if it feels absurdly
easy, then run what he has for the remaining 4km and ignore the band if he is better than it. The
standing failure mode in this repo is the opposite — prescribed targets being treated as ceilings
when they were meant as floors — so this one is spelled out in `follow:`.

## Verification

- **Running time budget:** 236min of 600 — OK.
- **Per-session caps:** longest is the 58min TT, under `weekday_session_max_min: 60` and on a
  Saturday regardless. No `budget_exception` needed.
- **`m` means minutes:** `40m`, `28m`, `15m`, `12m`, `10m`, `5m`, `3m`, `2m`, `50m` are minutes;
  `20s`/`60s`/`90s` are seconds. **The only metre-scale distances are written as km** — `1km` and
  `4km` in the TT. No bare `m` is used for metres.
- **Manual start:** both sessions with sub-3min reps carry `manual_start: true`. Tuesday's
  threshold session has no short reps and needs none. The TT has **two**
  `until-lap` steps — one to position for strides, one to start the 5k on the line — each with a
  header containing `PRESS LAP` so `compile_guide.py` maps it to a cue instead of clipping it. That
  clipping is what ruined the 2026-09-17 practice 5k, where the instruction reached the watch as
  `Warm up`.
- **Instrument by rep length (`AGENTS.md` invariant 4):** the strides primer and the TT are
  `target_mode: pace`, `follow:` pace only — correct for 20s reps, and correct for the TT, where
  ZoneSense lags the opening of a ~27min effort badly. **Tuesday's threshold session is `effort`
  with ZoneSense as the instrument**, because its 10min reps sit well inside ZoneSense's ~2min
  window and **`zones.yml`'s `threshold: 5:25` is stale in a known direction** — it predates a
  100km race and 30 days off. ZoneSense reads today's physiology; the pace is a cross-check that
  loses any disagreement. The two easy runs are `effort` / `ZS Z1`.
- **Rest steps:** every recovery step has an explicit target — `10:30/km Pace`
  (`pace.walk_recovery`), which sits slower than `walk_max` 9:30 as it must.
- **Pace band placement:** flat strides `4:10-4:40` centre `pace.stride` 4:25. Easy steps are bare
  `7:00/km` because `easy_ceiling` is a ceiling. Threshold floats are `7:10-7:40/km` =
  `pace.jog_recovery`, written as a range with the number at the slow edge because a float is a
  FLOOR, not a target.
- **Lifting placement:** n/a, no barbell work this week.
