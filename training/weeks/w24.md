## The baseline measurement

**Sunday's run is the new starting figure for the goal metric.** The standing number is **48.6**
from the 2026-08-23 5k PR; the race on 10-17 read 48.0 and should be discounted (19h27 at 62% of
max HR, 96% of it in `ZS Z1` — a poor input for an estimator built on HR/pace coupling during
running). Both readings and the retrieval path are recorded in
[`.claude/skills/daily-brief/references/suunto-fields.md`](../../.claude/skills/daily-brief/references/suunto-fields.md)
§ The watch's VO2max estimate — **`workouts_extensions` returns 500s on this account; the number
lives in the SML header as `MAXVO2`.**

**AMENDED 2026-11-17 — Sunday did not happen and is not being made up.** The 90min was an easy run
with his wife; when it fell through he chose to skip it rather than reschedule (athlete: *"I was
going to just skip the 90 minute"*).

**CORRECTED the same morning, by the athlete:** *"the watch probably won't give a vo2 max for this
run, and if it does it's suspect. This won't be near VO2 max at all."* He is right, and
[`suunto-fields.md`](../../.claude/skills/daily-brief/references/suunto-fields.md) § The watch's
VO2max estimate already said it — the estimate is modelled from the HR/pace relationship during
running and **needs sustained running at a decent intensity.** That is the whole reason the 10-17
race reading is discounted. **An easy restart run is the same bad input in miniature.** A session
that was briefly described here as giving a "cleaner" reading than the 90min would in fact give a
meaningless one.

**So this week takes no baseline reading at all.** The standing figure stays **48.6 from the
2026-08-23 5k TT**, and the first high-quality reading of the goal metric is the **5k TT on
2026-12-05** ([w26](w26.md)), which is already designated for exactly that. If the watch does emit
a `MAXVO2` off a restart run, record it as a curiosity and do not compare it to anything.

**Expect the new figure to land below 48.6, and record it without flinching.** Thirty days with no
running is a real detraining effect and a low floor is a better starting point than a stale peak.
**The one way to ruin it is to run Sunday harder than easy** — that produces a number that no later
easy run can be compared against.

## Strength — the ITB protocol starts this week

**Athlete, 2026-11-14:** *"I need to incorporate it band long term fix efforts starting now. Ghost
train 2027 is in about 10 months."* So it starts here, in the restart week, rather than waiting for

**The protocol is [`strength/itb-protocol.md`](../../strength/itb-protocol.md).** It runs
continuously from now to Ghost Train 2027 — this week is simply its first week.

**Order of operations this week:**

1. **The four baseline tests first**, before any of the work — side-plank hold to failure L vs R,
   single-leg stance eyes closed, single-leg squat filmed from behind, hip-hike reps to failure.
   Write the numbers into `log/`. [`lessons.md`](../../races/2026-10-17-ghost-train/lessons.md) § 7
   makes measuring priority 2 precisely so there is something to re-test against.
2. **Then the six exercises, 3× this week**, ~15min each, never on consecutive days. The protocol
   file has the doses.

**No general strength this week.** Liftoscript still has nothing past week 11 and nothing is being
appended — the ITB work is not part of that program and does not need it.

**The filmed single-leg squat can overturn the whole plan**, and that is worth knowing before
starting: pelvic drop is the athlete's hypothesis, not established fact. If both sides look level
under load, the mechanism is something else.
