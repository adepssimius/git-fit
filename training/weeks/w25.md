# Block week 25 — Mon 2026-11-23 → Sun 2026-11-29 — VO2 CYCLE 1, WEEK 1 (strides, hills, steady finish)

**First week of VO2 cycle 1**, per [`training/block-vo2.md`](../block-vo2.md). Cycle 1 runs
Mon 11-23 → Sun 01-03, and weeks 1-2 are the ramp-in: **strides, hills and a steady finish this
week; a threshold session and the 5k TT next week; full intervals from week 3.**

**There is no base phase in this block and that is deliberate.** Athlete, 2026-11-14: *"Why base
weeks at all? I spend nearly 6 months doing base building last year."* He has the aerobic base;
**what he lacks is recent fast running** — the last of it was the 2026-08-23 5k TT — and easy miles
do not buy that. Strides, hills and a sustained ZS Z2 block do.

**The week has three fast touches, escalating in force and duration:** flat strides Tuesday
(neuromuscular, no metabolic cost), hill strides Saturday (same length, more force per stride),
and a 15min sustained ZS Z2 block on Sunday (the first continuous hard effort).

**ADDED 2026-11-14 after the athlete asked whether the ramp-in was "just strides."** It was —
about 4 minutes of fast running across a fortnight, which is far too thin for someone four weeks
off a 100km with a 4h19 hike in between. Sunday's steady finish and next week's threshold session
are the correction.

**If the left calf or achilles says anything after Tuesday, do four hill reps on Saturday instead
of six.**

**Nothing here is time-critical.** Thursday is Thanksgiving; move it to any hour, or to Friday.

**Everything is under an hour except Sunday, which is exactly an hour** — so the ITB needs no
thought this week. It has never flared on anything shorter (athlete, 2026-11-14).

## Verification

- **Running time budget:** 234min of 600 — OK, with large room.
- **Per-session caps:** longest is 60min, exactly at `weekday_session_max_min`; it falls on a
  Sunday regardless. No `budget_exception` needed anywhere.
- **Instrument by rep length (`AGENTS.md` invariant 4):** 20s strides → pace only. Sunday's 15min
  ZS Z2 block is far longer than ZoneSense's ~2min window, so ZoneSense is the instrument there and
  pace is a cross-check. **That is deliberate: every quality pace in `zones.yml` dates from August
  and is stale in the same direction**, so for anything ≥9min ZoneSense is the only
  self-calibrating option until the 12-05 TT resets the table.
- **`m` means minutes:** `40m`, `30m`, `35m`, `60m`, `2m`, `5m` are all minutes; `20s` and `60s`
  are seconds. **No bare `m` is used for metres anywhere** — the only distances in the week are
  the `distance_km` frontmatter estimates, which are not steps.
- **Manual start:** both strides sessions carry `manual_start: true` and a
  `Strides positioning — find flat ground, PRESS LAP when ready` step ending in `until-lap`.
  (Saturday's is a `Hill positioning` step.) Required for any repeat block under 3min
  (`rules/endurance-authoring.md`), and the athlete's
  standing instruction from 2026-09-15: *"Never build a plan with strides that does not have a
  manual button push to start. I need to position myself."*
- **Stride band:** both stride sessions are `target_mode: pace`, `follow:` says pace only. The two
  pure-easy runs are `effort` / `ZS Z1`.
- **Rest steps:** every recovery step carries an explicit target. Stride walk-backs are
  `10:30/km Pace` = `pace.walk_recovery`, which sits correctly **slower** than `walk_max` 9:30.
- **Pace band placement:** `4:10-4:40/km` centres `pace.stride` 4:25 — strides are a TARGET, so
  the band is centred rather than bare. Easy steps use a bare `7:00/km` because `easy_ceiling` is
  a CEILING and downward expansion encodes it correctly.
- **Lifting placement:** n/a, no barbell work this week.
