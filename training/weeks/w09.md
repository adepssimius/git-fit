## Why this week looks like this

**This is a re-entry week, not a deload.** It was originally authored as a down week, inheriting
Champion week 6's character ("dialing back stress a bit... slight overreach on the down week for
adaptation"). The athlete corrected that on 2026-08-08: block week 9 immediately follows a
**zero-running vacation week** (~07-23 to 07-30, `log/2026-08-03.md`), so the recovery a down week
exists to provide had already happened — a deload stacked on a detraining week is redundant rest.
The conservative volumes here are **ramp-back-in caution after a week off** (plus absorbing the
unplanned 08-02 30k, TSS 307), which is a different reason with different consequences: nothing in
this week should suppress volume "for adaptation," and the calendar mapping to Champion week 6
survives only because the date alignment drives the Big Day and mid-cycle rest downstream.

No hard interval work — Wednesday consolidates what
Runna had split across two midweek quality days (`seed/runna-plan.md` week 9: Broken Miles +
Tempo 2km) into one moderate progression run, and volume everywhere else stays conservative.
Saturday's long run is continuous — **no walk breaks**. An earlier version of this week opened the
block's "run/walk protocol"; the athlete reversed that on 2026-08-04 and it is now race-day only
(`training/block.md` rule 6).
