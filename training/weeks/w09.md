## Why this week looks like this

overreach on the down week for adaptation"). No hard interval work — Wednesday consolidates what

Tempo 2km) into one moderate progression run, and volume everywhere else stays conservative. The
run/walk protocol starts **this week**, deliberately early, per `training/block.md` rule 6 — it
has to be automatic by race day, which means starting now, not waiting for the big peak weeks.
