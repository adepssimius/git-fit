## Why this week looks like this

**Three changes, all made 2026-09-07 with the athlete.**

**1. Saturday is the Big Day, recovered — a true 50k, not a lap sim.** It started as lap sim 1
pulled forward from 09-19 (possible work travel), then grew when the athlete asked whether he
needs a single 50k before race day. He does, and the date argument was his: **a 50k on 09-26
surfaces hour-5 problems exactly once and never retests them.** Run on 09-12 the retest slots are
09-19 (contingent), 09-26 and 10-03 — three, two certain. Finding a problem is half the value;
having time to fix it and test the fix is the other half. It absorbs what lap sim 1 was for, and
the 110min easy long run takes the 09-19 slot.

**2. Friday's past-midnight night run is folded into Saturday rather than run separately.** The
lap sim now starts at dusk and spends ~3 of its 3.5 hours in the dark, so it delivers the night
stimulus, the lap rehearsal and the lighting retest in one session instead of three. That makes
this weekend **cheaper** than the original plan, not dearer: Fri 90 + Sat 110 + Sun 60 = 260min
across three days becomes 207min in one. Family time is the reason, and it is the right reason.

**3. Tuesday is a 15k, at the athlete's request** — it was a rest day. See the budget note below;
it does not fit the weekday cap and is flagged rather than hidden.

**The lighting retest is the highest-value thing in this week.** 2026-09-06 killed a flashlight
and one of two headlamps inside 4h26 of dark, against ~12.1 hours on race night.
