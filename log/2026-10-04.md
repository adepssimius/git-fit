### ⚠ THE LADDER'S HRV BANDS DISAGREE WITH THIS ATHLETE'S OWN BASELINE — fix needed

**Athlete, 2026-10-04: *"Suunto shows my HRV as red."* He is right and the brief's first two reads
were wrong.**

`rules/progression.md` scores HRV on fixed absolute bands — green ≥80, amber 60-80, red <60. Those
are generic. **`athlete/zones.yml` already records his personal normal as
`hrv_rmssd_typical: 81-108`**, so a 79 is *below the floor of his range* while scoring "amber,
comfortably mid-band" on the ladder. The Suunto app compares against his baseline and calls it red.

**For this athlete the absolute bands are mis-calibrated low: an 80 is green on the ladder and
subnormal for him.** Scored against baseline, today is -13% on a 7-night mean of 91
(95, 84, 90, 89, 88, 100, 90).

**Proposed fix (not yet made): score HRV as a percentage of his own rolling 7-night mean** — e.g.
green within 7%, amber 7-15% down, red >15% down or below the `hrv_rmssd_typical` floor — and have
`brief_context.py` compute and print it so the brief cannot read the wrong instrument again.

### ZoneSense CANNOT be checked from the API — he asked, and the answer is no

**Athlete: *"You can check zonesense to see where I was truly."* Then: *"I see zonesense in the
Suunto interface and I was watching it on my watch."* Both true — and it is still not reachable
from the API. The evidence, because this will come up again:**

**The complete `workouts_sml` schema is 22 fields.** Established properly by dumping **360 samples
(`downsample 25`) of a full workout** — enough coverage to catch every field type present, unlike
a first attempt at `downsample 400` that saw only 37 samples and whose "no alpha-1 under any name"
claim was not supported by what had actually been checked:

```
TimeISO8601  AbsPressure  Altitude  Cadence  Distance  Power  SeaLevelPressure
Speed  Temperature  VerticalSpeed  EHPE  EVPE  NumberOfSatellites
Satellite5BestSNR  GPSAltitude  Latitude  Longitude  UTC
BatteryCharge  BatteryCurrent  BatteryVoltage  Events
```

- **No DFA a1, no R-R / inter-beat intervals, no ZoneSense zone.** A probe of 14 candidate names
  (`DFA`, `DFAa1`, `Alpha1`, `ZoneSense`, `RR`, `IBI`, `HRV`, `Rmssd`, `Intensity`, …) returned
  **0 samples**.
- **`HR` is almost absent from SML** — 12 sparse samples on today's run, none at all in the 1-in-25
  dump of the 10-01 run. The dense heart-rate stream that alpha-1 would need is not uploaded.
- **`workouts_extensions` returns HTTP 500 on every workout tried**, not just this one
  (`d3bqbagbfcelnoct` and `6c3oq49pof1eniml`, on `IntensityExtension` and `SummaryExtension`).
  **The endpoint is broken for this account**, so it cannot even be ruled in as the right home.
- **The only ZoneSense-derived figure that exists server-side is session-level: `DYNAMIC_DFA`
  TSS = 69.7** (against 66.4 from HR). One aggregate, not a zone trace.

**Conclusion: ZoneSense is computed on the watch, shown live, and rendered in the app — and never
lands anywhere the API exposes.** So **time-in-`ZS`-zone is a signal only the athlete can supply**,
in the same category as soreness and `shade_pct`. **Ask him for it; never promise to check it.**
