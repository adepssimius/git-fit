## Two analysis errors, both mine, both now guarded

1. **Read `VerticalSpeed` from the SML and reported the strides as flat to slightly downhill.**
   That field under-reports by ~4x. Corrected by differencing `Altitude` against `Distance`.
2. **Anchored fixed 20-second windows on each rep**, which manufactured a monotonic pace ramp that
   does not exist — real rep durations are 17-23 s.

Both are written into `.claude/skills/daily-brief/references/suunto-fields.md` so the next session
does not repeat them.
