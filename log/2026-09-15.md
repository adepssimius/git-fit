## Hip flexors came through the strides clean

**This morning's brief called the strides the first fast running since the 50k and named the hip
flexors as the tissue they would expose** — they were the standout soreness site on 09-13 and
20s at 4:20/km loads them hard. Athlete after the session: **no hip flexor issues.**

**That is a real data point and it is not the answer.** It rules out a hip flexor that is still
irritable enough to complain at speed, two days after soreness 8. It does not settle the open
question from `log/2026-09-12.md`, which is whether 7h14 of walking-strategy running found a
**durability gap** or simply a **novel stimulus**. Strides test neither — they are 100 seconds of
work in total.

**`endurance/2026-09-26-night-long-run.md` is still where that gets answered**, because duration is
the variable and only the long run supplies it.
