## What the restructure kept, and what it dropped

**Kept, and arguably better than the plan's own version:** the weekend's stated purpose was
"darkness PLUS deep fatigue PLUS sleep pressure," the race-lap-5-6 state. Running 20:15 to 00:34
puts four hours of genuine night running in the legs, then a bad night's sleep, then a second
long run on 11-hour-old legs. The Ghost Train runs through the night; rehearsing *into and
through* midnight is closer to that than a 03:00 start followed by going back to bed.

**Dropped:** the single 6h03 continuous block. The longest unbroken effort of this block is now
4h19m, against a race that will ask for far more, and the plan's one budget-flagged exception
existed specifically to buy that continuous time. Two runs of four and three hours with a night's
sleep between them is not the same stimulus.

**Unknown:** whether this was on the course, whether the three crew stops and the drop-bag pass
were rehearsed, and what the fueling was over 4h19m. None of that is in the data, and all of it
was a stated purpose of the session.
