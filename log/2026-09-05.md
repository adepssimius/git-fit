Every recorded signal said go, and he didn't want to. `rules/logging.md` says to record the call
actually acted on and treat the disagreement as the interesting part — usually that means the
table says green and the body says no, and the body is right. **Here the body said no and was
wrong**: he ran twice his intended distance at RPE 2 and enjoyed it.

Scope this correctly: it applies to a 5k he talked himself into, on an evening when the plan
played no part in the day. It is not evidence about how he responds to a session he knows is
scheduled.

That is a useful counter-example to have on file, because the block is heading into a stretch
where reluctance and genuine fatigue will be hard to tell apart. On this evidence, "don't feel
like it" on a fully green morning is worth testing with a short easy run rather than treating as
data. **The 5k floor is the mechanism** — a small commitment that can be extended, not a session
that can fail.

**Honest limits on this.** Publishing is clearly not *sufficient* — 08-16 and 08-17 were both

only as a file in a git repository.

**This is a different failure mode from everything else in this log.** Every other gap here is
readiness, tissue, illness or travel — things the plan is designed to absorb. This one is
delivery. The plan was authored correctly and never reached the athlete.
