**Monday was executed correctly** — 7:48/km at 131bpm is genuinely aerobic, and running slower
than the 7:00 ceiling the day after a 30k is the right call.

**Sunday was run far too hard for a long run.** Avg 159 for 3h31 is threshold-adjacent, not
aerobic; max touched 187, above the 186 previously stored as max. TSS 307 in a single session,
three days off a week of zero activity.

This is the single most useful thing learned so far, because it *independently confirms* the
speed/endurance gap inferred from the PRs and identifies the cause. Long runs run at ~85% max
build a strong 10k and a stalled aerobic base — the opposite of what a 100-miler needs. See
`athlete/zones.yml` → `long_run_hr_ceiling` (140). Expect pace to drop to 8:00/km+ initially to
hold that; it will come back down as the aerobic base builds.
