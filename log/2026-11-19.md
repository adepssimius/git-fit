## The 07:30 brief could not score this, and was right not to try

At the 07:30 pull **no sleep record for 11-18→19 had synced** — duration, quality, HRV and resting
HR were all absent, and the brief left `readiness: null` and asked, per the skill's rule that a
ladder missing most of its rungs is not a ladder. The athlete asked for the re-pull at 15:12.

**The draft reading off the resources curve was directionally right and 40min wrong.** The brief
inferred a ~02:00-02:15 sleep onset from balance pinned at 0.10 with `stressState` 2-3 through
02:00, flipping to `stressState` 1 and climbing from 02:30. **Actual onset 01:35.** Useful as a
tell, not accurate enough to score — which is the argument for asking rather than inferring.

**And this was NOT the 2026-11-15 false alarm**, which is the other half of the lesson. On 11-15 the
early pull read 4h13/red and the complete night was 8h34/green. Here the early pull was thin for the
same reason — he was still asleep — but **the completed night is genuinely amber.** A sync delay
means the data is early; it does not mean the night was good.

**Nothing was cut and nothing could have been** — today is a prescribed rest day
([w24](../training/weeks/w24.md)). Amber's instruction is to keep easy runs easy, and Saturday's
40min already is.

**Two consecutive ambers (11-17, 11-19) with 11-18 unscored between them.** Normally a re-author
trigger; not here, because both are bedtimes and a 120min restart week is not the thing to
re-author over sleep debt. **If 11-21 comes in amber too, the conversation is about bedtime.**
