## Finding: two exercises had no way to record both sides, and the fix is now live

Investigated after the athlete flagged it. Root cause, confirmed by comparing to how Single Leg
Deadlift behaved (it auto-split into `3x10|10` — left/right — despite being authored identically,
`3x10` with no `|`):

- **Per-side rep tracking only exists for certain BUILT-IN Liftosaur exercises.** Single Leg
  Deadlift has it; the custom "Single Leg Calf Raise" doesn't, because `create_custom_exercise`
  has no field to request it — not a setting we missed, the option doesn't exist for custom
  exercises.
- **Timed holds have no per-side tracking at all, built-in or not.** Copenhagen Plank's `30s|30s`
  is `setTimer|restTimer` (hold 30s, then rest 30s) — never a left/right split. That's very
  plausibly why it read as "very difficult": there was no second field waiting for the other side,
  so today's single 30s/side entry likely captured only one side, or was interpreted as the total.

**Fix, verified in `run_playground` before pushing:** doubled the set count on both exercises —
`Single Leg Calf Raise 4x12 → 8x12`, `Copenhagen Plank 3x1 30s|30s → 6x1 30s|30s` — which matches
the per-leg/per-side volume that was always intended (4 sets/leg, 3 holds/side) while giving each
side its own field. `Nordic Curl` gets a couch-anchor note: hook feet under a couch or other heavy
furniture instead of a bench. All three changes are live in Liftosaur now, effective next Lower B
(Thursday 08-13). See `strength/notes.md` § Substitutions for the full writeup.

One cost worth flagging: Lower B's estimated time went from ~36min to ~50min, purely from the
extra rest-timer transitions (14 more total sets, same total work per side). Still under the 60min
session cap, but with less slack.

## Finding: an unlogged walk, and a real scheduling conflict it exposed

Athlete asked to check for elevated step count in the last ~90min rather than logging a walk
directly. `wellness_activity` (10min buckets) shows a clear signal: **181 → 383 → 208 → 236 → 564 →
198 → 220 → 587 → 0 → 17** steps from 19:00 to 20:30, i.e. sustained elevated activity from ~19:00
to ~20:10 (**~80min, ~2,577 steps**), tailing to near-zero by 20:20. Consistent with an unlogged
walk during this evening's meetings.

**Cadence flag, worth being honest about:** ~32 steps/min average across the window is well below
a normal walking cadence (~100-130/min). This reads more like intermittent pacing during calls —
standing, short walks, sitting back down — than one continuous brisk walk. Real time on feet, but
probably not "brisk, sustained" per the walking style in `athlete/profile.md`. Credited as activity;
not asserting it was a formal walk.

**The conversation that started this surfaced a real bug, now fixed.** Athlete: "I can't do walking
AND bike trainer in the same day, I generally have 90-120 minutes of meetings." The daily-brief
script had been dividing the weekly walk target across all 5 weekday working days, including
Thursday — which every week of the block dedicates entirely to the 90min meeting-time trainer ride
(`endurance/*-meeting-zone2-ride.md`, always Thursday). That overstated available walking capacity
on every other day. Fixed: the script now excludes trainer days from the walking-day set entirely
(a trainer day contributes zero walking minutes, not a reduced share), so this week's 180min target
divides across **4 days** (Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri) at 45min/day, not 5 at 36min/day. `athlete/profile.md`
now documents the constraint and its consequence for the peak weeks — block weeks 16-17 come out to
116min/day across only 4 days, right at the top of the stated 90-120min budget with no slack, and
block week 14 (which also contains Labor Day) drops to just 3 walking days at 118min/day. Neither
is broken, but neither has room to absorb a bad week either.

## Sleep synced late — and the timing lesson is worth keeping

The first pull this morning had no entry for last night; it arrived on a retry. Worth knowing that
`wellness_sleep` can lag the morning brief, and that the honest move is to say the call rests on
partial data rather than treat absent as green.

The night itself is the best of the block: **7h56 unbroken, quality 0.85, HRV 96 (window high),
RHR 40, deep sleep 1h49** — recovered from 1h14 the night before.

The cause looks like session timing. Sleep onset moved 01:05 -> 00:07 because Wednesday's run went
to the gym at 15:25 instead of the usual post-bedtime slot. `training/block.md` justifies night
running partly as free family time, and it is — but it costs sleep onset, and this is the first
clean side-by-side showing how much. Two nights is not a finding; if the pattern repeats it is an
argument for putting quality sessions in daylight where the calendar allows.
