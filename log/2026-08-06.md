## Sleep synced late — and the timing lesson is worth keeping

The first pull this morning had no entry for last night; it arrived on a retry. Worth knowing that
`wellness_sleep` can lag the morning brief, and that the honest move is to say the call rests on
partial data rather than treat absent as green.

The night itself is the best of the block: **7h56 unbroken, quality 0.85, HRV 96 (window high),
RHR 40, deep sleep 1h49** — recovered from 1h14 the night before.

The cause looks like session timing. Sleep onset moved 01:05 -> 00:07 because Wednesday's run went
to the gym at 15:25 instead of the usual post-bedtime slot. `training/block.md` justifies night
running partly as free family time, and it is — but it costs sleep onset, and this is the first
clean side-by-side showing how much. Two nights is not a finding; if the pattern repeats it is an
argument for putting quality sessions in daylight where the calendar allows.
