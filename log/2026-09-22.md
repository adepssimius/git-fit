## Hip flexor eccentrics — bout 1 happened today, at 3x the proposed dose

**3 sets of 12 weighted throws per side**, the loaded leg-drop his mother designed
(`strength/notes.md` § Hip flexor eccentrics). The graded plan proposed a 6-rep unweighted primer
on 09-27 and the full 12 on 10-05; he did 36 weighted reps per side as a first exposure the same
day it was described. **Athlete: *"I'll be fine by Friday."***

**On the timing he is probably right.** Eccentric DOMS peaks 24-48h — Wed and Thu — and Friday's
03:00 night run is three days out, past peak.

**The risk is not soreness on Friday, it is stride.** Sore hip flexors change how the leg swings,
and Friday is four hours of swinging it. That is a different exposure from quad DOMS, which only
makes a run uncomfortable.

**It confounds nothing worth having** — Friday reaches ~28.8 km against a ~50 km onset, so it was
never going to read the hip flexors.

**He wants two more bouts** (athlete, 2026-09-22), so the series is three: 09-22, then the two
scheduled in `strength/notes.md` § Hip flexor eccentrics.

**What it changes going forward: 10-05 is no longer bout 1.** A 3x12 weighted first bout is
almost certainly enough to confer the repeated-bout protection by itself, so the objective is met
if this is tolerated. 10-05 becomes optional reinforcement, and markedly less soreness from it is
the protection showing up rather than a sign it did not work.

**The one thing to distinguish:** diffuse ache across the front of the thigh is the intended
outcome. **Sharp or localised pain in the hip crease is not** — that is strain territory, it is the
known mechanism of this exercise, and it would change what Friday should be.

**The presses land 3-13s BEFORE the speed stream shows the rise**, which is the belt ramping up.
That is why a speed-threshold read of the same session returns 10-18s reps against his stated
20-25s: **thresholding catches the rep mid-ramp. His durations are right and the derived ones are a
floor.**

**On the 9 mph: the lap `Speed.Max` values agree exactly with the speed stream** (3.80, 3.56, 3.28,
3.65, 3.48), so the gap to 9 mph (4.02 m/s) is the watch's treadmill speed estimate, not a sampling
artifact. At 11% the stride shortens and a wrist estimator calibrated on flat running under-reads.
`athlete/profile.md` already records that he calibrates treadmill distance after the fact.

**HR max 176 on 20s reps at 11%** — real work, and within reach of his 5k TT max of 186. Time above
HR Z4 totalled 4 minutes across the whole session.
