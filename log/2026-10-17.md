## What the fade actually was — the ITB, start to finish

**CORRECTION, athlete 2026-10-18.** This session first read the lap times as a blister story. They
are not. In his words: *"The lap 3 blisters weren't really a concern. I didn't feel anything off
after taping really and I wasn't limiting myself because of it. What happened is that after I got
to the DPW in lap 4 my IT band pain got worse and I was unable to run any longer. I walked the
back half of lap 4 and beyond."*

So the whole curve is one injury:

| Phase | What the ITB was doing |
|---|---|
| 0 → 4h19 | quiet. Lap 1 at 3h37, 8:10-8:12/km, the only lap run as written |
| **4h19 (27.3 km)** | **flares.** Tylenol + Voltaren, and the protocol list starts: walk the downhills, shorter steps, trail camber, wider stance |
| 4h19 → 15h15 | **manageable** — laps 2 and 3 at 4h27 and 5h00. The ~30 min/lap cost is the price of walking every downhill, not of the feet |
| **15h15 (84.7 km)** | **gets worse at the lap 4 DPW. Running stops entirely** |
| 15h15 → 19h27 | **walked**, to the end of lap 4 and the 3.2 km to 100 km |

**The blisters cost two tape stops and nothing else.** The 22-minute left-big-toe stop at 6h03 was
14 minutes over budget and bought a toe that never bothered him again — on his own account the
taping worked, both times. **Treat tape-early as proven, not as a cost.**

**The ITB arrived at 4h19 at 27.3 km** — again. `race.md` § Goal tiers said every failure found in
this block *"arrived at or before ~4h30, which is only evidence that the map ends there."* **The
race extended the map to 19h27, and the ITB still showed up at 4h19 — then gave eleven more hours
of running-and-walking before it took running away at 15h15.** The protocol list is what bought
those eleven hours and it should be read as validated.

### What RPE 4 means here

**He rated 19h27 and 100 km a 4, and said the effort was the knee, not the engine:** *"the real
effort was persisting through the knee pain. It felt good."* That lines up exactly with the watch —
avg HR 119, 62% of max, no excursions. **Aerobically this was never the limiter.** The honest
reading of the 100-mile tier is therefore not "not fit enough": it is that the ITB stops the
running at ~15h, and 100 miles needs a body that can still run after that. **That is a
rehab/strength problem, not an endurance problem**, and it is the thing the next block should attack.
