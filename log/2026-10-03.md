## ITB — left side, DURING, at 7 km. The distance rule does not explain this one

**`athlete/profile.md` § ITB puts onset at 20-25 km**, five kilometres early on 08-15 after time at
LTHR. **Today it appeared inside a 7 km session.** That is far outside the recorded pattern on
distance alone.

**The mechanism explains it, and it is the same mechanism.** ITBS is driven by hip adduction plus
internal rotation *during stance* — pelvic drop with the stance knee falling medially. **Downhill
running loads exactly that**, at higher knee flexion and with far greater force per step than flat
running. **So the trigger is not distance, it is accumulated stance-phase control failure**, and
403 m of descent run at the collapse limit buys that in 74 minutes what 20-25 km of flat buys
slowly.

**It resolved after, which matches every previous episode** and is the reason this is a note and not
an alarm. **DRAFT until he confirms the reading.**

**What it is worth keeping:** this is the first episode in the log with a *short* session attached,
so it is evidence that the 20-25 km figure in `athlete/profile.md` is a flat-ground number rather
than a property of the ITB. Race day is a flat rail trail, so the distance figure is still the one
that applies on 10-17.
