Three things fall straight out of laying pace against effort:

- **Km 4 was the crisis, and it shows in both columns at once** — the only km that lost time
  (5:15, 5s off plan) is also the first one to reach 8. Effort up, pace down: the textbook
  third-quarter sag, arriving exactly where it always does.
- **And then km 5 was the fastest split of the day.** A 5:01 *after* the worst patch, at 9 going
  to 10. Whatever that is, it isn't a pacing observation — he had a gear left after the point
  where the run stopped being fun, and he used it.
- **The easy parts stayed genuinely easy: 3, which is exactly the expected value for easy
  running.** On a day with a PB in it. `athlete/zones.yml` calls easy-day creep this athlete's
  most likely training error, and the temptation is never higher than on a day the legs feel
  good — so a 3 on the warmup and cooldown of a PB day is worth more than it looks.

**What it says about threshold (5:25), carefully.** The opening 2km ran 14–18s/km FASTER than
prescribed threshold pace and rated 6 — which is precisely the expected RPE for a tempo session.
That corroborates 5:25 as not-too-aggressive. It is **not** grounds to move it faster: the first
two km of a rested 5k flatter any pace, and threshold is defined by what holds for ~an hour, not
ten minutes. The trigger to revisit 5:25 stays what `zones.yml` already says it is — actual
threshold sessions reading 7–8 against an expected 6.

## METHOD CORRECTION — session-RPE was being time-weighted, and should not be

Logged here per `rules/logging.md` § Traps: a past mistake gets a correction dated today, not a
silent rewrite of the entries it affected.

**What happened.** Asked to rate this session, the athlete proposed **8**. This session's first
draft argued him down to **5** on the grounds of consistency with `log/2026-08-15.md`, which had
logged a 240min long run with 4x6min threshold embedded as a **3**, stating in its own frontmatter
that "3 is the time-weighted session figure." His objection is the correct one and is now the
repo's position: *"using it incorrectly in the past should result in correcting the past usage,
not polluting present data."* Consistency with a broken method is not a virtue — it is how one
bad reading becomes a trend.

**The method is gestalt, not arithmetic**, and `rules/logging.md` now says so explicitly rather
than leaving "rated after it settles" to be interpreted. The tell that settles it: this repo's own
expected value for an `intervals` session is 7, and no interval session can *time-average* to 7
once warmup, floats and cooldown are in the mean. A method that cannot reach the table's own
numbers is the wrong method.

**The artifact it produced.** Every rated session in the block has come in at or under plan, and
that has been read as a fact about the athlete:

| date | type | logged | expected | gap | method |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 08-05 | tempo | 5 | 6 | −1 | unstated |
| 08-09 | long | 3 | 5 | −2 | unstated |
| 08-12 | tempo | 3 | 6 | −3 | unstated |
| 08-15 | long | 3 | 5 | −2 | **confirmed time-weighted** |

Only 08-15 states its method, so it is the only one *confirmed* contaminated — the other three
fit the pattern and are suspect, but suspicion is not evidence and they are not being rewritten.
**No replacement numbers are being invented for any of them**: backfilling a rating from memory is
forbidden by this file's own rules, and would be a worse corruption than the one being fixed. The
practical consequence is that **the RPE trend restarts here, on 08-23, in gestalt units**, and the
four earlier readings are not comparable to it.

**A decision downstream of this needs flagging.** `athlete/zones.yml` moved threshold 5:40 → 5:25
partly on "the RPE ledger": plan-set sessions read −1/−2/−3 while the one he paced himself read 0.
But that "0" was the RPE **of the 6-minute reps**, while the −1/−2/−3 were **whole-session**
figures. Those are different quantities, and the ledger compared them directly. The conclusion
happens to have survived — tonight's 25:44 independently corroborates 5:25 — but it survived on
the pace evidence, not on the ledger. Flagged in `zones.yml` rather than acted on.
