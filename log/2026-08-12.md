Nothing has come in over plan since logging began, and today is the largest gap yet. Two
earlier entries already flagged this direction (`log/2026-08-09.md` § RPE trend, and the
08-05 finding that a prescribed tempo produced HR Z2). What today adds is **execution data
that rules out the obvious confound**: he did not rate it 3 because he ran it soft. He ran
the reps 6s/km faster than prescribed, with no fade, in unremarkable conditions, and still
never reached threshold HR.

**What this does and does not support.** The honest claim is bounded:

- It DOES support the threshold anchor having moved. `athlete/zones.yml` sets 5:40/km as
  "= 10k PR pace ... this IS ~1-hour pace", and sub-LTHR heart rate across seven reps at
  5:34 is hard to reconcile with 5:40 still being a genuine one-hour effort.
- It does NOT prove a new threshold pace. 2min reps with 90s floats are not a one-hour
  effort — anyone can hold well under threshold pace for two minutes. Seven of them at
  5:34 is evidence, not a test.
- It says least about the ENDURANCE end, which is the part `zones.yml` itself flags as
  extrapolated ("extrapolation flatters runners whose long-run volume is limited"). Today
  is 10k-adjacent fitness; it tells us little about 30-hour pacing.

**Nothing moves yet, and that is deliberate.** `zones.yml` § pace_review sets `next_review:
after the Big Day, 2026-09-05`, and the file's standing caveat is that the endurance end
needs real long-effort data before it is touched. Four under-plan RPEs are not that data.
The right next reads are Saturday's 213min with 4x6min embedded at 5:55 — a longer rep at
a slower target, which probes the same question with more duration behind it — and then
the Big Day.
