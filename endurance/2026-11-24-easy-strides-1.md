  then 30 days of nothing. Strides are the cheapest possible reintroduction: 20 seconds is too
  short to accumulate any metabolic cost, so the entire stimulus is neuromuscular and tendon.

  This is the whole reason cycle 1 has a two-week ramp-in rather than starting with intervals
  (training/block-vo2.md). Easy running does not prepare a tendon for interval speed; a stride does.
