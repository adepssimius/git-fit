## Pacing card — 29:30, km splits (2026-09-17)

**He runs the pace, she stays with him.** Splits in km; he is comfortable enough at 5:54/km to
read his own watch without it costing anything.

| km | split | cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **6:00** | 6:00 |
| 2 | **5:56** | 11:56 |
| 3 | **5:54** | 17:50 |
| 4 | **5:52** | 23:42 |
| 5 | **5:48** | 29:30 |

Sums exactly. Four seconds of slack in km 1, six spent in km 5.

**The failure mode inverts when he leads.** She cannot go out too fast because he sets the pace.
What can happen instead is that he holds 5:54 cleanly while she drifts a step off his shoulder in
km 3 and neither notices until it is a gap she has to close. **Two instruments: the watch for the
pace, her breathing for whether the pace is right.** Ragged before km 3 means 29:30 is the wrong
number for the day, and *where* it went ragged is the data point — not the finish time.

**What it decides for 10-11:** comfortable at 3 km, sub-30 is safe on race day and 29:00 is worth
discussing. Struggling at 3 km, the race target becomes a flat 30:00, and better to know it now
than six days out.
