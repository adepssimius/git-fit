Zone 2 means working, not racing — you should be able to finish it and want another five minutes.
intent: >
  The week's first sustained hard effort, and the answer to the athlete's own question on
  2026-11-14 — *"Are those workouts just strides?"* They were, and 4min of fast running across a
  fortnight was too thin for a man four weeks off a 100km.

  A steady finish on the back of an easy run is the gentlest way to reintroduce sustained work:
  the effort is continuous rather than repeated, the force per stride is nowhere near interval
  level, and it comes at the end when the legs are already warm.

  **ZoneSense rather than pace is the point here.** Every quality pace in zones.yml dates from
  August, before a 100km and 30 days off, so all of them are stale in the same direction.
  ZoneSense reads today's physiology instead of August's, which makes it the only self-calibrating
  instrument available until the 12-05 time trial resets the table.
