intent: >
  The first genuine workout of the block, and the session that makes the 12-05 time trial worth
  running. Added 2026-11-14 after the athlete asked whether the ramp-in was *"just strides"* — it
  was, and a 5k TT off nothing but 20s reps produces a soft number. Since that number sets the
  interval paces for weeks 3-6 of this cycle, a soft one would propagate into four weeks of
  training.

  **Threshold rather than intervals, deliberately.** It is continuous rather than repeated and the
  force per stride is far below VO2max work, so it is the right first hard session three weeks off
  a layoff — and it is the specific preparation a 5k needs, since a 26min 5k is run near threshold
  rather than near VO2max.

  **ZoneSense rather than pace**, for the same reason as 2026-11-29: every quality pace in
  zones.yml predates a 100km race and 30 days off. ZoneSense reads today's physiology. The whole
  point of the TT four days later is to replace those numbers.
