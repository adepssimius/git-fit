# The Champion Plan for 100 km to 100 miles

Verbatim, as supplied by the user (professionally authored, Roche-style mountain ultra plan).
Frozen reference — do not edit. This is the **structural authority** for block weeks 9–19, per the
week-mapping table in `training/block.md`. Where it conflicts with the user's own rules (time
budget, strength spec, flat-course adaptation), the user's rules win — see
`training/block.md` § "The Champion Plan — what gets adopted, adapted, and dropped" for the
resolution.

> Note: the distance ranges are designed to create multiple plans in one. Low end of the range:
> intermediate; high end: very advanced athletes with big base.

---

## Week 1

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal, like hot tub or sauna 20-30 min. Light x-train or a hike is good on any
rest day. Full rest with mobility work and core is fine too. Do the Core Snack routine 1-2 times
every day!

**Tue** — 6-12 miles easy plus 6 x 20 second hills fast. On easy running, think smooth, relaxed,
and light on feet. Ideally Z1/Z2, but it's ok for HR to get higher on ups. Do easy runs by effort,
and it's ok to flow when it flows! On the hill strides, do them anytime after halfway, easing into
them to avoid a pulled muscle, but push as fast as you can go without using sprinting form. Jog down
the hill for recovery. If you have a treadmill, add 10-15 minutes on the uphill treadmill after any
run at 10-15% grade for fatigue resistance — can be weight vest hiking as well.

**Wed** — 2-3 miles easy warm-up, 6 x 90 second hills mod/hard (think 5k effort) with run down
recovery after each, 6 x 45 second hills faster (think 3k effort), 1-2 miles moderate (think
marathon effort), 1-2 miles easy. The hills will engage top-end power, the moderate running after
will help the body shuttle lactate at higher speeds.

**Thu** — Optional easy double or x-train. Any double can be the uphill treadmill, or a hike with a
weight vest. Doubles are 25-45 min, with 30 min a sweet spot, and they can be VERY easy. If your
race involves lots of hiking, weight vest hiking at 15-20% grade would be ideal as you get closer to
race day, working on muscular endurance. Light Strength (like Mountain Legs), right after you finish
your run on strength days. You can do a different routine that works for you as well (like Ultra
Legs), or add squats.

**Fri** — 1-2 hours easy x-train with 8 x 30 seconds fast/2 min easy. X-train days are ideally bike,
elliptical, uphill treadmill running, weight vest hiking, stairmill, or a combination. Aim to keep
easy in Z2 rather than extremely low effort. On fast pushes, use a bigger bike gear or higher
elliptical resistance and push! Sub in a very easy run if 100% healthy, or split into a double.
Usual Tuesday mileage ranges are ideal if running.

**Sat** — 4-10 miles very easy. Aerobic recovery day! Only advanced athletes should go farther than
6 miles, since you want to be fully recovered for the weekend. Heat ideal.

**Sun** — 10-16 miles easy/mod, progressing to steady effort. It's ok to let effort wander up.
Practice fueling with plenty of carbs and hydration! Some vert is good, but choose whatever route
seems most fun. Do long runs on terrain with climbing and descending — vert is your friend, and more
important than mileage in most cases. Think quick feet on downhills, using every downhill as an
opportunity to work on that skill. Mountain Legs.

*(Sunday is sometimes a flex day — long bike, ski, or hike, or mix a run and uphill treadmill. On
Sundays, add 2 x 10 reps of squats and back extensions (2 x 20), engaging glutes, to Mountain Legs.)*

## Week 2

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal. Very advanced athletes could jog, only if super high mileage 80+ mpw.

**Tue** — 6-14 miles easy with 5 x 20 seconds fast/2 min easy. Ease into these flatter strides even
more since injury risk is higher. Top of the range is only for high-mileage athletes; most will
thrive in the middle, particularly the day before workouts.

**Wed** — 2-4 miles easy warm-up, 16 x 1 min fast/1 min easy (think 5k effort), 2-3 miles easy/mod
to mod (think 50k), 1-2 miles easy. Speed workout classic — building speed stimulus. Post-run
uphill treadmill 10-15 min is ideal after workouts.

**Thu** — Optional easy double or x-train. Mountain Legs.

**Fri** — 1-2 hours easy x-train with 5 x (4 x 1 min fast/1 min easy) with 2 min easy between sets.
Building x-train power before it becomes mostly aerobic later. If doing uphill treadmill or jogging,
skip the structured workout, just unstructured.

**Sat** — 4-12 miles very easy. Heat ideal — passive heat is low stress, add freely, but not on
double days.

**Sun** — 13-18 miles easy/mod (20 min mod/hard around 1-hour effort near the start). Think relaxed,
permission to push a bit more near the end. Tempo best on a net uphill to reduce impact forces.
Mountain legs + squats + back extensions (strength a day earlier when Tuesday has a workout the
following week, but flexible with timing).

*(Monday-equivalent easy day: 6-14 miles very easy plus 6 x 30 second hills fast or easy/mod x-train
— stress of the first 2 weeks adding up. Mountain Legs.)*

## Week 3

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal.

**Tue** — 2-4 miles easy warm-up, 2-3 x (4 x 2 min fast/1 min easy) with 2 min easy between sets
(think 10k to 5k effort), 2 miles moderate (marathon), 1-2 miles easy. Big and hard session — the
range of sets means lower-volume athletes don't overdo it. Goal is not max effort but smooth,
finishing feeling strong. Err on the side of less intensity. Optional easy double or x-train.
Mountain Legs.

**Wed** — 6-14 miles easy plus 6 x 20 second hills fast. Keep very easy post-workout.

**Thu** — 1-2 hours easy x-train with 3-4 x 3/2/1 min fast with 1 min easy between intervals and 3
min easy between sets. Final tougher x-train workout for a bit as the body adapts. Mountain Legs.

**Fri** — 4-12 miles very easy. Heat ideal.

**Sat** — 16-20 miles easy with 1 mile harder near the end. Pure aerobic day, faster mile is a
fatigue-resistance stimulus. Fuel like the race to work on fatigue resistance — aim for 75+ g
carbs/hr. Mountain Legs.

**Sun** — 8-14 miles easy or easy/mod x-train — could be long adventures/hikes. Mountain legs +
squats + back extensions.

## Week 4

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal.

**Tue** — 8-14 miles easy with 5 x 30 seconds fast/2 min easy. Ease into strides, smooth and
strong.

**Wed** — Uphill treadmill at 8-10% ideal (can be done outside, cut intervals in half for downhill
running): 15-20 min easy warm-up, 12-15 x 3 min around 1-hour effort with 90 seconds easy recovery,
15-20 min easy cooldown. Uphill treadmill threshold allows big sessions with little impact/risk —
key to this plan. If outside, err toward more relaxed. Optional easy double or x-train. Mountain
Legs.

**Thu** — 1-2 hours easy x-train. Quality Z2 if non-impact (cycling). Good time for more weight-vest
uphill hiking if racing with lots of hiking. Mountain Legs.

**Fri** — 6-14 miles very easy plus 5 x 20 second hills. Heat ideal.

**Sat** — 12-18 miles easy/mod with 20 min mod/hard around 1-hour effort, finishing harder (tempo
near the start after warm-up, plus 5 min harder near the end). Taxes glycogen reserves — fuel with
major focus. Mountain Legs.

**Sun** — 10-16 miles easy, progressing effort to steady, plus 6 x 30 second hills at the end, or
easy/mod x-train. Mountain Legs + squats + back extensions.

## Week 5

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal.

**Tue** — 8-14 miles easy with 5 x 20 seconds fast/2 min easy.

**Wed** — 2-3 miles easy warm-up, 3-5 x 800/400/200 fast on track with 400 easy after 8s, 200 after
4s, 400 after 2s (10k/5k/3k effort), 4 x 200 fast/200 slow (push), 2-3 miles easy cool-down. First
track workout. Off-track alternative: 3-4 x 3/2/1 min fast with 1 min easy between intervals and 3
min easy between sets, plus 4 x 30 seconds fast/90 seconds easy. Optional easy double or x-train.
Mountain Legs.

**Thu** — 1.5-2.5 hours easy x-train. Mountain Legs.

**Fri** — 6-14 miles very easy. Heat ideal.

**Sat** — 16-20 miles easy/mod (1 hour mod to mod/hard, starting at marathon effort and progressing
— the "power hour" will be a bit tough, embrace it, best translation to race readiness). Training
races can replace long runs any week if available — keep Friday shorter that week. Mountain Legs.

**Sun** — 10-16 miles easy, pure recovery style, light on feet and relaxed. Mountain Legs + squats +
back extensions.

## Week 6

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal.

**Tue** — 6-12 miles easy plus 5 x 20 second hills. Aerobic down week ahead, dialing back stress a
bit.

**Wed** — 10-13 miles easy/mod to mod, progressing effort, 2 miles easy cool-down. Settling into
steady running — key for longer races. Optional easy double or x-train. Mountain Legs.

**Thu** — 1.5-2.5 hours easy x-train. Mountain Legs.

**Fri** — 4-12 miles very easy with 5 x 20 seconds fast/2 min easy. Heat ideal.

**Sat** — 10-16 miles easy over hills. Pure aerobic day, nice and relaxed. If race has a ton of
vert, start focusing on vert profiles like the race. Mountain Legs.

**Sun** — 8-14 miles easy over hills or easy/mod x-train. Pure aerobic day. Mountain legs + squats +
back extensions.

## Week 7

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal.

**Tue** — 8-14 miles easy with 4 x 20 seconds fast/2 min easy.

**Wed** — 2-3 miles easy warm-up, 2-3 x (4 x 600 fast with 200 easy between each) and 600 easy
between sets (10k effort to start, progressing). Finish with 4 x 200 fast/200 slow, pushing, and
2-3 mile easy cooldown. Off-track: 2-4 miles easy warm-up, 8-10 x 2 min fast (10k) with 90 seconds
float recovery, 2 miles steady (50k), 1-2 miles easy. Optional easy double or x-train. Mountain
Legs.

**Thu** — 1.5-2.5 hours easy/mod x-train — solid aerobic stimulus. Mountain Legs.

**Fri** — 6-14 miles very easy. Heat ideal.

**Sat** — 16-20 miles easy/mod with 4-7 x 6 min around 1-hour effort with 2 min easy recovery. Big
threshold session, ideally on rolling terrain. Err on the easier side for true threshold. Mountain
Legs + squats + back extensions.

**Sun** — 10-16 miles easy plus 4 x 30 second steep hills at the end or easy/mod x-train.

## Week 8

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal.

**Wed** — Uphill treadmill at 8-10% ideal (or outside, halve the intervals): 15-20 min easy warm-up,
8-12 x 4 min around 1-hour effort with 90 seconds easy recovery, 15-20 min easy cooldown. Optional
easy double or x-train. Mountain Legs.

**Tue** — 8-16 miles easy plus 6 x 30 second hills.

**Thu** — 1-2 hours easy x-train. Nice recovery before the bigger weekend run. Weight vest hiking
would be great if the race involves lots of hiking. Mountain Legs.

**Fri** — 6-14 miles very easy plus 5 x 20 second hills. Heat ideal.

**Sat** — 20-25 miles easy/mod with some very fast downhills on steep terrain, plus 5 min harder
near the end on an uphill. Aim to get sore from this one — dialing in eccentric contractions good
for durability. If doing major vert, even advanced athletes can be at the low end of the range.
Mountain Legs + squats + back extensions.

**Sun** — 10-16 miles easy/mod flow on flatter/rolling terrain, practicing settling into a Z2/Z3
rhythm, or easy/mod x-train.

## Week 9

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal.

**Tue** — 8-14 miles easy with 5 x 20 seconds fast/2 min easy. Dial back volume this week if
feeling beat up — trying to have a slight overreach here for adaptation on the down week next week.

**Wed** — 2-3 miles easy warm-up, 8-10 x 800 fast with 400 easy recovery after each (10k effort to
start, pushing a bit as you go and ending harder), 1-2 miles steady, 1-2 miles easy. Quality track
session before down week. Off-track: 2-3 mi easy warm-up, 5-8 x 3 min fast (10k effort) with 2 min
easy recovery, 1-2 mi steady, 1-2 mi easy. Lactate shuttling and speed tuning day, 10 days out from
a solid effort. Mountain Legs + squats + back extensions.

**Thu** — 1.5-2.5 hours easy/mod x-train. Mountain Legs.

**Fri** — 6-14 miles very easy with 5 x 20 seconds fast/2 min easy. Heat ideal.

**Sat** — 12-20 miles easy. Very easy and relaxed, pure aerobic activation. Mountain Legs.

**Sun** — 6-14 miles easy plus 6 x 30 second steep hills at the end or easy/mod x-train. Mountain
Legs.

## Week 10 (taper into training-race weekend)

**Mon** — Rest or easy jog (jog is fine to keep volume higher). Heat ideal.

**Tue** — 2-3 miles easy warm-up, 3 x 5 min around 1-hour effort with 1 min easy recovery, 5 min
easy, 4 x 45 second slight hills fast (3k effort) with 75 seconds easy recovery, 2-3 miles easy
cool-down. Light tune-up pre weekend effort, practicing the taper. Mountain Legs.

**Wed** — 5-10 miles easy. No strength the rest of the week.

**Thu** — Rest and recovery. Go into the big weekend effort feeling recovered and ready.

**Fri** — 3-8 miles easy to steady plus 4 x 30 sec light hills around 3k/5k effort. Heat ideal.

**Sat** — **BIG DAY!!!** Can be a marathon, 50k, or even 50 mile race. If not racing, aim for a
moderate 50k on terrain like the race, pushing moderately hard with full fueling approach. Can move
this backward to 8-10 weeks pre-race if there's a race or 100k — move around weeks accordingly.
Goal: get very sore! Weeks 10 and 11 can be moved to accommodate a training race.

**Sun** — CELEBRATION! Should be quite sore in a good way. Eat a lot the next few days.

## Week 11 (mid-cycle rest)

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal.

**Tue** — Rest. Mid-cycle rest block, down week ahead to solidify gains. Heat ideal.

**Wed** — 4-10 miles easy. Really ease back in. Start focused heat suit training now if the race
might be very hot and you aren't running in that heat consistently — 2 x 30-60 minutes with a heat
suit each week, keeping HR in Z2 or easier. Don't fry yourself. Heat suit can be on doubles or
replacing easy running.

**Thu** — 1.5-2.5 hours easy/mod x-train with 10 x 30 seconds fast/2 min easy. Mountain Legs.

**Fri** — 4-10 miles easy.

**Sat** — 10-16 mi easy. Pure aerobic day. Heat ideal.

**Sun** — 8-14 mi easy plus 4 x 30 sec hills fast near the end or easy/mod x-train. Get to the end
of this week feeling fresh. Mountain Legs + squats + back extensions.

## Week 12

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal.

**Tue** — 8-14 miles easy with 5 x 20 seconds fast/2 min easy.

**Wed** — 2-3 miles easy warm-up, 3-4 x (4 x 400 fast) with 1 min between intervals and 2 min
between sets (5k effort), 1-2 miles moderate (marathon), 1-3 miles easy. Track workout to re-ignite
speed mid-block. Off-track: 2-4 miles easy, 16 x 1 min fast/1 min easy (5k effort), 2 miles moderate
(marathon), 1-3 miles easy. Might feel a bit tired here. Optional easy double or x-train. Mountain
Legs.

**Thu** — 1.5-2.5 hours easy x-train. Mountain Legs.

**Fri** — 6-14 mi easy plus 4 x 30 second hills.

**Sat** — 16-20 mi easy/mod with 20 min mod/hard, pushing a bit as you go. Fuel like race day or
more — start really gut training, particularly with large amounts of fluid. Mountain Legs + squats +
back extensions.

**Sun** — 10-18 mi easy plus 8 x 30 sec steep hills at the end or easy/mod x-train. Fatigue
resistance hills. Mountain Legs.

## Week 13

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal.

**Wed** — Uphill treadmill at 8-10% ideal (or outside): 15-20 min easy warm-up, 2-4 x 10 min around
1-hour effort with 2 min easy recovery, 2-4 x 5 min around 1-hour effort with 2 min easy recovery,
15-20 min easy cooldown. If outside: 4-6 x 5 min hills with run-down recovery between each. Optional
easy double or x-train.

**Tue** — 10-18 miles easy plus 6 x 20 second slight hills. No strength mid-week now as running
stress gets higher.

**Thu** — 1.5-2.5 hours easy/mod x-train.

**Fri** — 4-12 miles easy. Heat ideal.

**Sat** — 20-25 mi easy/mod to mod on steeper terrain with some fast downhills and 5 min hard near
the end on tired legs. These harder downs help prevent excess muscle damage from derailing the race.
Steeper the better (within reason), even for flat races. Mountain Legs + squats + back extensions.

**Sun** — 10-20 mi easy, progressing to steady over rolling terrain.

## Week 14

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal.

**Wed** — Uphill treadmill at 8-10% ideal (or outside): 15-20 min easy warm-up, 8-12 x 5 min around
1-hour effort with 1 min easy recovery, 15-20 min easy cooldown. Big one, embrace the space. If
outside: 5-8 x 5 min hills, stepping up from last week. Optional easy double or x-train. Mountain
Legs.

**Tue** — 10-20 miles easy. Aerobic endurance day, ideally Z2 rather than pure Z1. Optional easy
double or x-train for high volume athletes.

**Thu** — 1-2 hours easy x-train. Mountain Legs.

**Fri** — 6-14 miles easy plus 5 x 20 second hills. Heat ideal.

**Sat** — 14-18 mi easy/mod to moderate (60-90 min mod/hard — a nice effort to open up the nervous
system). Only go beyond 1 hour if high volume, feeling great, and recovering well. If too tired,
dial back and keep to 12 miles easy, starting the taper earlier. Mountain Legs + squats + back
extensions. **Final squats day.**

**Sun** — 10-16 mi easy or easy/mod x-train. Pure recovery mode, light on feet.

## Week 15 — taper begins

**Mon** — Rest. Heat ideal.

**Tue** — 6-10 mi easy plus 5 x 20 sec hills fast. Starting the full taper, keep it relaxed going
into Wednesday's workout.

**Wed** — 8-12 mi easy/mod over hills. Nice flow run, with serious vert if the race is steep.
Optional easy double or x-train. Mountain Legs.

**Thu** — 46-60 min uphill treadmill easy Z2 or easy x-train 45-60 min. Pure aerobic day.

**Fri** — 3-6 mi easy. Heat ideal.

**Sat** — 10-14 mi easy/mod over hills — final verty run if the race is steep. Really practice GI
training here, fueling like the race or more. Mountain Legs.

**Sun** — 4-8 mi easy. Pure chill day.

---

## Week 16 — race week

**Mon** — 4-10 mi easy Z2.

**Tue** — 2-3 miles easy warm-up, 3 x 5 min around 1-hour effort with 1 min easy recovery, 5 min
easy, 3 x 30 second slight hills fast, 2-3 miles easy cool-down. Repeating the tune-up workout used
earlier in the cycle. Mountain Legs to stay tuned up.

**Wed** — Uphill treadmill Z2 40-60 min or 4-8 mi easy. Pure aerobic day.

**Thu** — Rest and recovery. Light heat (sauna/hot tub) good for blood volume.

**Fri** — 3-6 miles easy to steady plus 4 x 30 sec light hills around 3k/5k effort. Z2 running
ideal for the tune-up. HR will be jumpy and that's ok.

**Sat** — **100 Mile or 100k Race!** Think Z2 effort, fuel and hydrate plenty, and most of all:
BELIEVE. You got this!

**Sun** — **100 Mile or 100k Race! Celebration time!**
