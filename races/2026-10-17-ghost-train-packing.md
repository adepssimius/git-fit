# Ghost Train 2026 — packing list

Tick it off race week. Where things came from and why: `races/2026-10-17-ghost-train.md`
§ Drop bags and carry and § The full night system. What is in each product: `rules/products.md`.

**Counts assume up to 7 laps** (100 mi is ~6.7 laps of 24.1 km).

## Before you leave home

- [ ] Watch charged to full Friday night. Don't run with it Friday
- [ ] Phone charged
- [ ] All 18650 cells charged — lights, restock piles, and the 2 that go in the power bank
- [ ] **Zip tie on every charged cell.** No zip tie = used. It slides off the end, no
      cutting — only when the cell goes in a light or the bank
- [ ] Nitecore NU25s charged
- [ ] Feet pre-taped on clean dry skin: big-toe callus margins and 2nd toes. Lube around the tape,
      not under it
- [ ] Packets checked: they say **SALTY**, not regular Mortal
