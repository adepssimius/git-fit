# Ghost Train 2026 — pacer checklist, tent and DPW

Go through it with him at both ends, out loud, top to bottom. He does the work; you fetch from the
tent or the drop bag if he asks.

- [ ] *[not on the card]* **Tell him at 5 minutes and at 10 minutes.** He needs to know if he's spending too long.
- [ ] *[not on the card]* **Flask** refilled with water.
- [ ] *[not on the card]* **Sodium check — too much water first.** Urine clear and frequent, plus puffy hands,
      headache, nausea or bloating? Then no water until he's peeing less, and salt without water:
      salty food off the table or SaltStick FastChews. **No packet in the flask.**
- [ ] *[not on the card]* **Sodium check — otherwise,** 450 mg sodium (9 FastChews) over the next leg, if any of
      these is true:
  - [ ] He skipped the Tailwind at both aid stations since the last end.
  - [ ] He's cramping. Pickle juice too.
  - [ ] He's craving salt.
  - [ ] Hot afternoon and his sweat is leaving salt crust.

  None true → no packet.
- [ ] *[card 3 · FUEL]* **Solid food** eaten off the aid table. Ask: "have you eaten in the last 40 minutes, and did
      it stay down?"
- [ ] *[not on the card]* **Pee:** ask when he last went and what colour. Over 2 hours ago → he drinks a full flask
      before leaving. Clear and under an hour → skip the extra water.
- [ ] *[card 8 · LIGHTS]* **After dark: fresh cell in his Zebralight**, every leg, even if it's still working.
- [ ] *[card 8 · LIGHTS]* **Power bank on him with two charged cells in it** — those are his spares. Loose spares
      are zip-tied; no zip tie means used.
- [ ] *[card 3 · FUEL]* **5 gels** on him when he leaves.
- [ ] *[card 4 · FEET]* **Feet:** ask about hot spots, blisters or numbness. Shoes come off only if he says so.
      Blister kit and spare socks are at both ends.
- [ ] *[card 5 · ITB]* **ITB:** ask if he needs to manage it, and if he wants Tylenol or Voltaren gel. That's the
      whole question — no talk of stopping over it. See [knees](protocols.md#knees) and [meds](protocols.md#meds).
- [ ] *[card 9 · CHAFING]* **Chafing:** ask about his armpits first — they're the spot that has chafed (skin on skin,
      44 and 48 km on 09-12). Then nipples, waistband, anywhere else rubbing or sore. If yes,
      [chafing card](protocols.md#chafing). Aquaphor/vaseline is at both ends.
- [ ] *[card 4 · FEET]* **Shoes:** ask if he wants to swap shoes.
- [ ] *[card 7 · WARM ENOUGH]* **Layers — tent only.** If the next lap runs into dark or colder weather, he takes the
      layers for the whole lap now. At DPW there are none: if he's cold, get him moving.
- [ ] *[this is the self check card]* **Lap 4 on: read the SELF CHECK CARD out loud with him.**
- [ ] *[card 6 · SLEEPY OR STUPID, and card 7 · WARM ENOUGH]* **Watch him while he does all this:** slurring, clumsy hands, shivering, limping, can't keep
      food down. Say what you see.
- [ ] *[card · TWO RULES]* **Walk him out. BEN MAY NOT DECIDE TO QUIT AT A STOP.** No quit talk until 20 minutes
      down the trail.
