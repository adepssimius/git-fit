# THE CARD — Ghost Train 2026

### AT EVERY BAG

**1. TIME AND PLACE.** Say the clock time and where you are.
*Can't say it → you're worse off than you feel, and your answers below can't be trusted. Eat, caffeine.*

**2. PACE.** Cutoff 3:00pm Sun (30h). 100km = 100. 100mi = 160.9 km.
*Pace needed = time left ÷ km left.*

**3. FUEL.** Eaten in the last 40 min, and did it stay down?
*No → solid food now, standing. Walk out eating.*

**4. FEET.** Any hot spot, blister or numbness?
*Hot spot → tape now. Blister → `athlete/profile.md` § Blister kit. Numb, or gait changing → stop the lap.*

**5. ITB.** Whisper, or changing how I run?
*Need meds?*

**6. SLEEPY OR STUPID?** Tripping, missed turns, lost minutes?
*Caffeine, 20 min walking, real food. You are not sleeping this race.*

**7. WARM ENOUGH?** Shivering, or hands can't work tape?
*Layer on BEFORE you need it.*

**8. LIGHTS.** Both working? Two charged cells in the power bank?
*Restock the bank to two at EVERY bag. Fresh cell in the chest light.*
*Zip tie = charged. No zip tie = used.*

**9. CHAFING.** Armpits? Anything rubbing or sore?
*Aquaphor.*

### TWO RULES

**STIFF AFTER SITTING IS NORMAL.** It has eased within about 20 minutes of moving every time. Don't decide to quit while it lasts.

**DECIDE AFTER 20 MIN OF PROGRESS AWAY FROM THE BAG. NEVER MID-LAP.** Leave the bag, move 20 min, then decide. Finish the lap you're on.
